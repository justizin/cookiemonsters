City council approves the new waterfront bike lanes

After two years of debate, the 4-mile route along the bay will open next spring.

By A. Reporter · 6 min read

The council voted 9–2 on Tuesday night to fund the protected lanes, which will connect the ferry terminal to the north beaches. Supporters packed the chamber; opponents worried about parking.

Construction is expected to begin in January, with the first segment opening before the summer tourist season.
